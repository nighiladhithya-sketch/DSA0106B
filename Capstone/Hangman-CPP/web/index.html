How to play

  • Guess the hidden word one letter at a time — click the keys or type on your keyboard.
  • Pick a word length (3, 4, 5 … 9+ letters) or Any on the start screen.
  • You get 6 wrong guesses. Each one draws the next part of the hangman: head, body, left arm, right arm, left leg, right leg. The sixth opens the trapdoor.
  • Know the word? Type it in the Solve box. A wrong solve counts as one wrong guess.
  • Hints: reveal a letter or show the clue. Each costs one hint and 15 points.
  • Easy: 3 hints, clue shown · Medium: 2 hints, ×1.5 points · Hard: 1 hint, 90-second timer, ×2.5 points.
  • Score: 10 per unique letter + 20 per life left − 15 per hint, + 2 per second left on Hard, × difficulty multiplier.
  • Streak bonus: each consecutive save adds 10% (up to 50%). Skipping or giving up resets it.

Shortcuts: A–Z guess · Enter next word after a round · Esc close dialogs
